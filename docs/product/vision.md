# Product baseline v0.1

## Elevator pitch
Today's AI chat tools are designed for conversations, not for thinking. When exploring complex problems, thinking is rarely linear: we branch into questions, visualize relationships, experiment with ideas and bring insights together. Yet most AI chat tools constrain this process to a linear stream of messages. Thinkspace is an AI Thinking Workspace that enables people to explore, visualize, connect and converge on ideas through graph-based conversations, interactive generative interfaces and composable context. Instead of simply chatting with AI, users can think with AI.

## Vision and positioning
Make AI-assisted thinking as natural as human thinking itself. Intelligence augmentation rather than delegation-first automation. The product is not merely a ChatGPT alternative or a whiteboard. Its central job: when exploring a complex problem with AI, organize, visualize, experiment with and connect different lines of thinking so that users can form coherent understanding and informed decisions without losing context.

Initial audience: AI power users, architects, engineers, consultants, analysts and researchers; product managers and deep learners are adjacent segments. Market demand and willingness to pay are unvalidated.

## Principles
- Thinking-first: prioritize cognitive outcomes.
- Conversation-native: ordinary chat stays natural; no mandatory canvas management.
- Non-linear by nature: branch, revisit, converge.
- Visual when valuable: AI chooses appropriate representations; avoid decoration.
- Context by composition: explicit references, scope and provenance.
- Progressive organization: structure emerges as work evolves; default automatic layout.

## Core experience
Explore (chat/branch), Visualize (GenUI), Experiment (local interaction), Connect (references), Synthesize (merge), Reflect (overview/history). Core loop: Chat → Generate UI → Interact → Promote to Canvas → Compose Context → New Chat. Chat nodes contain linear messages; graph links organize topics. Artifacts are first-class and can become context sources. Graph proximity is not context inclusion.

## Hypotheses and validation
H1 graph organization reduces retrieval and context recovery time; H2 GenUI improves understanding accuracy and exploration time; H3 explicit context composition reduces omissions and repeated explanation; H4 persistent artifacts improve research resumption and reuse. Compare real complex tasks against native ChatGPT, measure effort, correctness, time and maintenance overhead. None are proven.
