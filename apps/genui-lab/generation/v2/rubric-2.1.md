# Evaluation Rubric 2.1

Score a delivered response or refinement turn independently. Preserve the first attempt, final disposition, repair count and denominator. Assign pass, fail, not applicable (NA) or not evaluated (NE) to each axis. NE includes insufficient evidence and ambiguous oracle bindings. Record a reason and observable evidence for every judgment.

| Axis | Evidence and decision |
|---|---|
| Transport | A complete envelope satisfies the version, shape and byte limits. Refusal, truncation or invalid structure fails delivery. A missing response is NE. For native ChatGPT the lab wire contract is NA. |
| Specification | A submitted UI passes existing admission and yields a valid initial Worker frame. Rejection fails. Plain text is NA and unobservable source is NE. Native hosted UI has no lab specification requirement. |
| Intent | The visible response answers the question or implements the requested change. Missing requested interaction fails. Undelivered content is NE. |
| Domain fidelity | Check rules, units, allowed options, defaults and claims. A concrete contradiction fails. B01 mathematical and B02 architectural keyword matches only identify review candidates. Correct mappings leave unexplained prose, ambiguous labels and extra measures or ranking tables pending content assessment. |
| Behavior | Compare observable outputs against independent expectations while varying inputs. Record states and counterexamples. A confirmed mismatch fails; unidentified roles or untested behavior are NE. Missing interaction is also captured under intent. Pure explanation is NA. |
| Presentation | Inspect rendered output at a recorded viewport. Check whether labels, units, hierarchy, contrast and grouping support reading the result. Record the visible evidence; absent inspection is NE. |
| Modality | Assess whether the chosen text, visual or controls serve the requested activity. Score independently of factual accuracy and visual polish. |

Core success requires delivery, passing intent and domain and, for an interactive request, passing behavior. NA and NE add no passes. Report the mapping denominator separately. For multi-turn work record prior behavior retained, new behavior, and state continuity separately. Failure to identify a plotted series is NE unless a visible contradiction or missing requested representation is established.

Record reviewer identity or pseudonym, case ID, rubric version, axis scores and rationale in a separate human scorecard. Keep automated results and prior ratings hidden until submission. A human must actually inspect the rendered outputs before attributing human ratings. For paired experience assessment also record the answer to the comprehension task, elapsed time, and preferred representation with a reason. A paired preference alone cannot certify domain or behavioral correctness.
