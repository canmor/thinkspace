# Independent interactive review

Assign anonymous participant numbers before scheduling. Alternate SVG-first and Chart-first for odd and even numbers. Reverse that order on the next task. Use the same device, viewport, task instructions and initial selections on both sides. Have each reader operate a live replay. Screenshots support later annotation. Keep this administrator sheet and the numeric answer keys outside the participant view.

## End-to-end availability

Pair runs by task, protocol and seed, retaining both failed and successful deliveries. Give each participant the same task question for each arm. Record whether a usable artifact is available, whether it has the requested controls and whether the reader can complete the requested action. If an artifact is unavailable, record availability as zero and mark the dependent timing and quality scores missing.

## Equivalent-function comparison

Before assigning a pair, check both completed artifacts against the frozen numeric oracle, labels, input domains and required interactions. List eligible pairs and excluded cases with reasons. For baggage ask the reader to select Economy, Gold, International and 38 kg, then find the difference between the proposed and existing fees. Repeat at 35 kg and explain what changed. For archive ask how many pages remain after six days at four pages per day, then change the pace to five. Ask the participant to show where they read each answer. Use the Goal 2B.2 addendum for the separate native-product pairing.
